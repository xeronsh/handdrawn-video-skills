# Xeron Black Cat IP, Visual Style DNA & Authentic AI-UI / Jargon Sketch Guide

## 1. Character Lock: Xeron Black Cat (`高冷极客黑猫`)

Every illustration stars the Xeron black cat as a deadpan, competent, expressive system operator with natural feline C/S-curve body language (never a stiff mannequin or corner sticker).

### Facial & Head Features

- **Fur & Silhouette**: Pure solid-black (`#0D0D0D`) hand-drawn cat head with clean, slightly organic pen outlines.
- **Ears**: Two sharp, upright triangular cat ears with **muted grey-purple (`#6E6378`) inner ears** and subtle two-tooth fur notches at the inner-ear base.
- **Signature Eyes**: Striking **bright mint-green (`#2EE59D`)** rectangular-almond eyes with **flat horizontal upper eyelids** ("下三白" half-lidded aloof geek stare) and vertical black pupils (or cool mint-rimmed dark geek shades during Vibe Coding).
- **Nose, Mouth & Whiskers**: Tiny dark-grey T-shaped nose, subtle deadpan/smug/skeptical mouth, and **two short dark-grey whiskers on each cheek**.

### Expressive Full-Body Acting Poses

- **Flexible C/S-Spine**: Lean forward in an arched C-curve when furiously typing, recline with coffee and `[Tab]` key when Vibe Coding, facepalm or scratch behind the ear when debugging hallucinations, yank a slot-machine lever when rolling prompts, or leap with tucked knees and a flowing S-whip tail on eureka moments.

## 2. Mode 1.0 (Default): Pure White Hand-Drawn Explainer

- **Canvas**: `16:9` horizontal (or `3:4` vertical cover) on a pure solid `#FFFFFF` background edge-to-edge (zero paper grain, zero beige cast, zero drop shadows, zero outer border).
- **Whitespace Budget**: Main drawn elements occupy 40% to 55% of the frame, preserving at least 45% open pure-white space.
- **Strict Color Coding**:
  - **Black (`#141618`)**: Primary line art, mechanical/UI props, hand-drawn icons, primary labels, and Xeron's solid-black body.
  - **Core Mint (`#2EE59D` / `#0D8E5F`)**: Hero highlights, active Tab/Cache/MCP badges, and Xeron's eyes.
  - **Secondary Marker Accents**: Orange (`#DE7A22`), Red (`#C93B35`), or Blue (`#2B548E`) sparingly for diffs, warnings, or status badges.

## 3. Mode 2.0 (Optional): Mixed-Media Real-Object Studio Scene

- Place 1 compact real photographic tabletop object (mechanical keyboard, ThinkPad laptop, crumpled notes, brass clip) on a clean `#FFFFFF` studio tabletop with soft natural contact shadows, while the flat 2D hand-drawn Xeron black cat physically interacts with it.
