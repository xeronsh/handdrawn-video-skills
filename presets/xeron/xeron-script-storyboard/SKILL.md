# Xeron Script Storyboard

Co-create authentic, logically airtight, Douyin-compliant video scripts and fast-paced 16:9 hand-drawn Chibi Xeron black cat storyboards through structured interviewing and metaphor-first causal auditing.

## Core Principles

1. **Preserve Authentic Geek Voice (Never Cliché Ghostwriting)**: Extract or audit the user's own thoughts without erasing their candid phrasing or closing catchphrases (strictly avoiding `——` dashes and preachy filler).
2. **Metaphor-First 1:1 Audio-Visual Parity & Single Scale**: Ensure spoken copy explicitly introduces the concrete visual metaphor shown on screen (`声画同构`, e.g., boiling a kettle of water, clearing a game boss pit, folding a `2-7` off-suit hand in Texas Hold'em), and eliminate conflicting dual scales (never mix invented `70%/80%` numbers with `100°C` boiling point).
3. **Fast-Paced Multi-Beat Sub-Scene Rhythm (`每幕 2–4 拍景别递进 + 气口横移转场`)**: Break every act into crisp `[拍1]..[拍4]` causal beats (`Setup -> Side-by-Side Proof -> X-Ray/Review Ledger -> Threshold Breakthrough`) using pure 2.5-head Chibi Xeron poses (avoiding props like crank bicycles that stretch chibi legs).
4. **Hand-Drawn Draftsman Callouts & Geek Easter Eggs (NEVER Pop-Up Term Cards)**: Pair visual cause-and-effect with curved-arrow hand-drawn callout notes (`手绘引线批注`) and corner Easter eggs (yellow Debug Rubber Duck, peeking mini cat, achievement toasts).
5. **Douyin Compliance Pre-Desensitization (`创作阶段前置排雷`)**: Rephrase gambling terms (`别赌博梭哈` -> `千万别上头梭哈`), proxy/IP digits, anti-fraud bypass (`防风控`), competitor platform names (`小红书/闲鱼`), or exploits (`漏洞`) before TTS synthesis.
6. **100% Verbatim Parity**: Every word in Section II act dialogue must match Section I teleprompter copy word-for-word.

## Steps

1. **Propose 3 to 4 Focused Metaphor Angles (or Audit Existing Draft)**: Check for scale clashes, delayed metaphors, detour sentences, and Douyin ASR/OCR risk words.
2. **Run a Concise Interview When Starting Fresh**: Ask 2 to 3 focused questions per round to capture real examples and boundaries.
3. **Choreograph Multi-Beat Chibi Watercolor Storyboard**: Design 2 to 4 `[拍]` beats per act with draftsman callouts, Easter eggs, and `Xeron` signatures.
4. **Output the Standard Two-Part Deliverable**:
   - `## 一、提词器纯净连读版（完整口播逐字稿）`
   - `## 二、完整视听分镜大纲（逐字稿 × 16:9 二头身水彩手绘画面、批注与彩蛋）`
